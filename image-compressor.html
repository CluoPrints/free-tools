Compress Image to a Target Size

Need a photo under 100 KB for a form, a listing or an email? Upload an image, pick your target size, and download a file that fits. Everything runs locally in your browser; your image is never uploaded anywhere.

Choose an image

Target file size

How it works: JPG and WebP images are re-encoded at the best quality that fits your target (within ±10% or 5 KB). PNG images are converted to JPG by default, because JPG compresses photos far better — use “Keep PNG” only if you need transparency. Animated GIFs are compressed as a single still image; animation is not kept. If your file is already smaller than the target, we will say so — no fake compression.
